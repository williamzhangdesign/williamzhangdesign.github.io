I The short version

I'm William. Today I lead the end-to-end bank application redesign, referrals CX, and AI scaling for designers at Capital One — before that, shipping across banking, credit cards, and strategy.

I studied at the University of Michigan, where I co-designed the MI Safe Start Map, the state's COVID dashboard, with UMSI and UMSPH. These days I'm also in graduate coursework at Georgia Tech, building AI-first healthcare concepts.

I use AI as a tool to make the processes between design, product, and engineering more efficient: study the problem deeply, form a research-backed strategy, and ship it with pixel perfection. The goal is always measurable, impactful results.
